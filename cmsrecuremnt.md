1. Project Background

Currently, candidate tracking is managed through Excel sheets, which creates challenges such as:
•	Duplicate candidate calls and follow-ups.
•	Difficulty tracking candidate history.
•	Lack of centralized candidate database.
•	Inability to identify previously rejected candidates.
•	No structured rejection reason repository.
•	No automated communication process.
•	Limited reporting and analytics capabilities.
•	Difficulty tracking offer acceptance and joining status.
To overcome these limitations, HexaLearn plans to customize the Hex App into a future-ready Candidate Management System (CMS) that centralizes recruitment operations, improves recruiter productivity, and enhances decision-making.

2. Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are:
1.	Eliminate duplicate candidate interactions.
2.	Maintain a centralized candidate database.
3.	Track complete candidate lifecycle.
4.	Improve recruitment efficiency.
5.	Generate meaningful reports and analytics.
6.	Maintain historical records of all candidates.
7.	Support future scalability and integrations.
